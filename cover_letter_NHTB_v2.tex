\textbf{Subject: submission of ``Symmetry-Selected Nonlinear Thermal Pumping in a
Periodically Heated Mixed-Convection Channel''}\\
\textbf{Article type: Research Article}\\[4pt]

Dear Editor,

We submit the enclosed manuscript for consideration in \emph{Numerical Heat Transfer,
Part B: Fundamentals}. Its argument rests on three mutually independent pillars: an exact
selection theorem, a nonlinear confirmation of that theorem by direct numerical
simulation, and the deliberate falsification of its own pre-registered error model, reported
as a result rather than smoothed over.

\medskip
\textbf{Thesis.} The paper establishes an exact symmetry-selection theorem for nonlinear
wall-flux redistribution in a periodically heated mixed-convection channel, and shows that
the \emph{direction} of the pumping is set by the Reynolds number
at fixed P\'eclet number. Separating the two is the physics: symmetry fixes the structure
of the response, $\Nu_{2,0}(+1)=-\Nu_{2,0}(-1)$ exactly for every admissible
$(\Rey,\Pr,\Grr,k)$ under the stated assumptions, while the coupled thermal--hydrodynamic
dynamics fix its amplitude and sign. A second exact result follows: under phase-shifted
modulation the rectified response carries two pumping coefficients, in-phase
and quadrature, of opposite sign and roughly twice the magnitude, so at equal wall
amplitudes it is proportional to $\sin\phi$ with no higher harmonic.

\medskip
\textbf{The headline number.} The rule was tested in its most destructive configuration,
equal wall amplitudes in phase, where the theorem forces the pumping to vanish identically.
Fully nonlinear DNS returns a wall-flux difference of $3.1\times10^{-18}$ against individual
fluxes of $1.15\times10^{-7}$: a relative cancellation of $2.7\times10^{-11}$, eleven digits
beyond the flux being cancelled.

\medskip
\textbf{Nonlinear persistence of the $\Grr$-independence.} Corollary~1 states that the
reversal point does not move with the buoyancy amplitude; verified perturbatively to
$1.2\times10^{-10}$ in $\Rey_c$, it is now tested in the fully nonlinear regime by a
four-run DNS Grashof pair. For runs differing in $\Grr$ by exactly a factor of four, the
measured pumping fluxes ratio to $4.0003$ and $4.0015$, and the DNS--perturbative
\emph{discrepancy} itself ratios to $3.996$ and $4.015$. Because the discrepancy scales with
$\Grr$ like the pumping does, the second-order correction rescales the response without
displacing its zero: the reduced coefficient $A=\Pi_T/\Grr$ at the two Grashof numbers falls
on a single branch. The residual $0.1\%$
and $0.4\%$ departures from exact linearity are not read as a Richardson-number
correction --- the two pairs order the reverse of what such a term predicts --- and are
reported as consistent with exact Grashof linearity at the tolerance the runs reach.

\medskip
\textbf{What the campaign falsified.} A pre-registered deviation model calibrated on an
earlier sweep is exceeded by five of the twelve non-degenerate cases. The manuscript
characterises the excess instead of widening the bound: it is independent of the forcing
amplitude and grows with the wavenumber, which we report as an empirical observation rather
than a scaling law. We do not attribute it to a mechanism, because the available checks do
not separate truncation from resolution; the refined-streamwise-mesh replica of the worst
case is named as the next test rather than asserted as a conclusion.

\medskip
\textbf{Positioning, and a disclosure.} The manuscript is the third member of a series on
the same canonical configuration. Study~1, the exact integral framework for the
\emph{symmetric} wall modulation, was submitted to this journal on 1 November 2026; study~2,
the corrected modal decomposition that establishes $\Nu_{2,0}=0$ in that symmetric case, is
likewise unpublished. Neither carries a citable identifier, so both are described in the text
rather than cited, and their full texts are available to you on request. The overlap is
stated up front because it is measurable: a word-level duplication scan returns three shared
twelve-word shingles with study~1 and none with study~2, all three inside the
author-and-affiliation block, and no scientific sentence is carried over; a footnote in the
formulation section repeats that accounting. The new physics of this paper is the asymmetric
configuration: the odd-parity selection rule, the two-coefficient phase law and the reversal
mapping $\Rey_c(k)$ at seven wavenumbers, with DNS confirmation at four of them.

\medskip
\textbf{Why this journal.} The contribution is methodological as much as physical, and no
layer of the evidence validates itself. The parity identity and the Green identity verify
the perturbative hierarchy --- adaptive collocation replicated by an independent fixed-mesh
shooting solver, field agreement $9\times10^{-12}$ --- while the fully nonlinear stationary
DNS, performed independently of those boundary-value solvers, tests the symmetry argument
rather than the solver that produced it. Residuals are reported quantity by quantity, an
error model is pre-registered before the campaign and then falsified by it, and candidate
designs are rejected beforehand by a resolvability margin, so no run is launched on a signal
it could not resolve. That combination --- an exact identity, a two-strategy verification
hierarchy and a documented failure of the author's own error estimate --- is fundamentals
material for the readership of \emph{Numerical Heat Transfer Part B}.

\medskip
\textbf{One terminological caution.} ``Pumping'' here is not the travelling-wave propulsion
of Hossain and Floryan, \emph{J.\ Fluid Mech.}\ \textbf{966}, A43 (2023): the observable is
the difference between the two wall heat fluxes at strictly fixed bulk flow rate, a
transverse redistribution with zero net transport.

\medskip
\textbf{What is not claimed, and reproducibility.} The results are two-dimensional, laminar
and restricted to the domain where the second-order expansion is quantitatively controlled;
no universality is claimed. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere,
and duplicates no text, figure or derivation from the companion studies. I disclose that its
source and PDF, with all solvers, campaign scripts, raw tables and figure generators, are
publicly deposited at \mbox{\url{https://github.com/Ahmedalgeria778/symmetry_selected_thermal_pumping}};
please judge that deposit against the journal's preprint policy. The submission is divided into
a main manuscript and a separately numbered supplementary document: the theorems and every
headline measurement stay in the main text, while the eight derivations, the parameter maps and
the supporting figures are gathered in the supplement under S-numbering, each cited and discussed
from the main text. The data arrive as
a supplementary package of 64 files, inventoried with the SHA-256 digest of every entry, from which one
consistency script regenerates every number quoted in the text.
