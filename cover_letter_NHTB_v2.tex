\textbf{Subject: submission of ``Symmetry-Selected Nonlinear Thermal Pumping in a
Periodically Heated Mixed-Convection Channel''}\\
\textbf{Article type: Research Article}\\[3pt]

Dear Editor,

We submit the enclosed manuscript for consideration in \emph{Numerical Heat Transfer, Part B:
Fundamentals}. The paper shows that the sign of the second-order wall-flux redistribution in a
streamwise-periodically heated mixed-convection channel is fixed by parity,
$\Nu_{2,0}(+1)=-\Nu_{2,0}(-1)$ for every admissible $(\Rey,\Pr,\Grr,k)$, while its amplitude
and its direction are set by the coupled dynamics: at fixed P\'eclet number the direction
reverses with the Reynolds number. A Green identity reduces the rectified flux to one integral
coefficient that scales linearly with $\Grr$, so the reversal point does not move with the
buoyancy amplitude. Phase-shifted wall modulation gives the response two pumping coefficients
of opposite sign whose sum is proportional to $\sin\phi$ at equal amplitudes. Every prediction
is checked against fully nonlinear stationary DNS, performed independently of the
boundary-value solvers that produced it. In five of the twelve non-degenerate cases of the
campaign the deviation exceeds the error bound specified before the campaign, and the excess
is reported and characterized.

\medskip
The work continues a line of research on the same canonical configuration. Its companion, the
exact integral framework for the \emph{symmetric} wall modulation, was submitted to this
journal on 1 November 2026 and is under review here. That study drives the walls in phase,
where the quantity that carries the present results vanishes identically, so no result, figure
or derivation is shared between the two papers.

\medskip
The submission consists of a main manuscript and a separately numbered supplementary document
whose figures use S-numbering and are each cited from the main text. The sources, solvers,
campaign scripts, raw tables and figure generators are deposited publicly at
\url{https://github.com/Ahmedalgeria778/symmetry_selected_thermal_pumping}; please judge that
deposit against the journal's preprint policy.

\medskip
The manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. I declare no conflict of
interest, and the work received no external funding.
